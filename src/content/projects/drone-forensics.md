## Project Overview

The Drone Forensics Laboratory explores techniques for examining
drone-generated digital evidence, including flight logs, GPS
coordinates, timestamps and telemetry.

The project will investigate how recorded flight data can be
used to reconstruct a flight and identify events requiring
further examination.

## Research Objectives

The primary objectives include:

1. Understanding common drone telemetry formats.
2. Identifying relevant evidence sources.
3. Preserving original flight logs and recording hashes.
4. Extracting timestamps and GPS coordinates.
5. Reconstructing flight paths.
6. Examining altitude, speed and battery information.
7. Documenting findings and limitations.

## Potential Evidence Sources

Drone investigations may involve:

- Aircraft flight logs
- Ground control station logs
- Controller data
- Mobile application databases
- Photographs and video metadata
- GPS and GNSS records
- Device configuration information

## Investigation Methodology

### Evidence Identification

Identify the drone model, associated devices and available
digital evidence.

### Evidence Preservation

Preserve original evidence and calculate cryptographic hashes
where appropriate.

### Data Examination

Extract relevant timestamps, coordinates, flight events
and telemetry values.

### Flight Reconstruction

Use recorded coordinates to reconstruct the flight route
and correlate events with other evidence.

### Reporting

Document observations, assumptions, uncertainty and
limitations.

## Research Ethics

All investigations should use authorised evidence and comply
with applicable laws and privacy requirements.

Publicly shared flight data should be reviewed to avoid
disclosing sensitive locations or personal information.
